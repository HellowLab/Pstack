# Show me your work

1. Choose an authorized scratch or project location. Use an ordinary Markdown table or TSV; no bundled executable helper is needed.
2. For each substantive decision record time, decision, reason, evidence, expected result, actual result, and next action. Separate observation from inference.
3. Include rejected alternatives, failures, changed hypotheses, and verified milestones. Keep entries concise and tied to real artifacts.
4. Keep private material local. Commit a sanitized trail only when the project's authorized delivery requires it. Never publish the conversation or credentials.
5. At handoff, link the trail and summarize the current state, exact artifacts, failed checks, and next step. Do not invent durable storage when files are unavailable.
