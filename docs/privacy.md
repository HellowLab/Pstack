## How Pstack works

Pstack provides engineering instructions and reference material for ChatGPT and Codex. Its skills-only distribution contains no MCP server configuration, executable helper, hook, bundled telemetry, or publisher-controlled collection endpoint.

Your host and authorized connected services process the prompts, code, files, history, and other information needed for your requested workflow. Workflows can create project artifacts, preferences, and decision logs. The providers’ processing, retention, and deletion follow their own policies and your account settings, separately from this policy. See [OpenAI’s privacy policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/).

## Support information

When you contact Pstack support, your message may include your name or account handle, email address, issue description, and attachments. Support information is used to answer requests and fix problems. It is never sold or used for advertising or model training by the publisher.

GitHub and the email provider process support communications through their services under their own policies. This policy describes the publisher’s handling of support information and does not replace those providers’ policies.

General support is available at [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/HellowLab/Pstack/issues). GitHub Issues are public and stay public unless removed. Do not post credentials, private code, or confidential information. See [GitHub’s privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Use me@seankudrna.com for private support or privacy requests.

## Retention and removal

Support information is kept only as needed to answer requests and fix problems. You can request removal by emailing me@seankudrna.com. Information will be removed on request where it is under the publisher’s control. This does not guarantee removal of copies outside that control, such as third-party copies, host records, or your project files.
