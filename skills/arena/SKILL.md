# Arena

1. Frame the artifact, shared brief, and three to six measurable criteria. Choose the number of candidates and assign isolated outputs.
2. Produce distinct candidates. Use actual permitted subagents when available; otherwise produce sequential candidates and disclose that they share one agent's context.
3. Read every candidate fully. Obtain a separate judge only when available and permitted. Score each criterion and record disagreement.
4. Pick the most maintainable base. Incorporate useful parts of other candidates deliberately; record what was rejected and why.
5. Verify the combined artifact. Report base, incorporated ideas, failures, and evidence. Do not imply model diversity or reviewer independence unless it actually occurred.
